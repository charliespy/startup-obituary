In January 2023, Jasper AI was worth $1.5 billion.

By summer, they were laying off a third of their staff.

The product hadn't changed. The market had.

Dave Rogenmoser built Jasper — originally Jarvis, until Marvel's lawyers came knocking — on a simple bet: most people don't know how to prompt an AI. So he built the interface. A friendly text box that turned "write me a blog post about SEO" into 800 polished words.

Marketers loved it. The numbers were staggering. $80 million in annual revenue. 700 employees. A roster of investors led by Insight Partners at a $1.5 billion valuation.

Then OpenAI released ChatGPT.

Not as a developer API. Not as a B2B product. As a free tool anyone could use. The interface Jasper had spent two years perfecting — the whole product — was now free. For everyone. Forever.

"We went from 'this is magic' to 'why am I paying for this' in about six weeks," a former sales lead wrote on Blind. "Enterprise clients started asking if they could just use ChatGPT instead. We didn't have a good answer."

The churn was brutal. Consumer subscribers left first. Then the small businesses. Then the mid-market accounts that were supposed to be the backbone of growth.

Jasper pivoted. The new pitch: they weren't a writing tool anymore. They were an "AI marketing platform." Enterprise workflows. Brand voice consistency. Team collaboration features. Everything ChatGPT couldn't do.

But the pivot came with a cost. The simplicity that made Jasper viral — paste a prompt, get content — was buried under enterprise features most customers didn't need.

The layoffs came in waves. First a quiet reduction. Then a louder one. The company still exists, still has revenue, still has customers. But the $1.5 billion valuation belongs to a different era.

An era that lasted about eleven months.

The lesson Jasper teaches isn't about AI. It's about building on rented land. When your entire product is a better interface for someone else's technology, you're one product launch away from irrelevance.

OpenAI didn't set out to kill Jasper. They just opened the door that Jasper had been charging admission to walk through.
