Stable Diffusion changed everything.

In August 2022, a startup most people had never heard of released an AI image generator that anyone could download, modify, and run on their own computer. No API calls. No subscription. No permission needed.

Artists were furious. Developers were ecstatic. And Emad Mostaque, the founder of Stability AI, became the most polarizing figure in the AI world overnight.

The pitch to investors was intoxicating: Stability would be the Red Hat of AI. Open-source the models, make money on enterprise services and compute. The market bought it. $101 million raised. A billion-dollar valuation.

There was just one problem.

The money was going out a lot faster than it was coming in.

Stability was burning $8 million a month. GPU clusters don't come cheap, especially when you're training models and giving them away for free. Revenue, meanwhile, was reportedly under $5 million annually.

"The business model was essentially: step one, open-source everything. Step two, ???. Step three, profit," a former employee told Forbes. "Nobody could explain step two."

Mostaque had a vision. Multiple visions, actually. Stability wasn't just images — it was audio, video, 3D, language models, biotech. A new initiative every month. Each one requiring more compute. More researchers. More money.

The researchers started leaving first. Some went to Midjourney. Others to Google. A few started their own companies. The people who had actually built Stable Diffusion were walking out the door, and the models that followed were noticeably worse.

In March 2024, Mostaque stepped down as CEO. The board had lost patience. The company he'd built had given the world an extraordinary gift — and had absolutely no idea how to pay for it.

Stability AI proved something the tech industry keeps forgetting: giving things away for free is not a business model. It's a marketing strategy. And marketing strategies eventually need a business attached to them.

The irony is almost too perfect. Stability gave the world the tools to generate anything. Everything except revenue.
